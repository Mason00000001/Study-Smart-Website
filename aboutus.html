Why do we do what we do?

We at the StudySmart company believe that all students deserve to stay hydrated and healthy during their studies at school.

We believe that young peoples health is one of the most important things in the world, so we produced our product to allow students to monitor their hydration intakes daily, be reminded to reach a daily goal, and of course stay healthy and hydrated.
