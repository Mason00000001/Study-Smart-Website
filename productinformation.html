Product Information

The StudySmart Smart Hydration Bottle helps users stay hydrated throughout the day with built-in hydration reminders and water-intake tracking. Its leak-proof lid makes it suitable for school, work, travel, and everyday use, while its insulated design helps keep drinks hot or cold for hours.

The bottle features the StudySmart logo and a modern design created to support focused, healthy routines.

What it is made out of

The bottle is designed with a durable, food-grade stainless-steel body, double-wall insulation, and a secure BPA-free leak-proof lid.
